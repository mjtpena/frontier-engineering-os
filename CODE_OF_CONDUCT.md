# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other channel that represents the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable.
- Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants who do.

## Reporting

To report a conduct incident privately, use the repository's private reporting form:
<https://github.com/mjtpena/frontier-engineering-os/security/advisories/new>.
Start the title with `[Conduct]`. Only maintainers can see these reports.

Reports are handled by the maintainer (@mjtpena) and kept confidential. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in the report.
Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
