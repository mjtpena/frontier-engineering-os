# L3 Domain — Australian Government (example)

> Example domain layer. A human domain owner MUST confirm the applicable frameworks and versions for each engagement before use.

- **Regulatory frame (confirm per engagement):** Protective Security Policy Framework (PSPF), ASD Information Security Manual (ISM), Essential Eight, Privacy Act 1988 (APPs), the Australian Government policy for the responsible use of AI in government, and agency-specific policies.
- **Data rules:** data residency in Australian regions; classification per PSPF markings; no OFFICIAL: Sensitive or above to services not assessed for that level.
- **AI:** AI transparency statement and accountable official requirements as applicable to the agency; human review of decisions affecting individuals.
- **Required reviews:** agency security adviser (ITSA), privacy officer for PII, records management for retention.
